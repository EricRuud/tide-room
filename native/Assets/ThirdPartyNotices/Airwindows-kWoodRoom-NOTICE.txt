Tide Room retains the upstream kWoodRoom DSP source in
native/third_party/airwindows/kwoodroom. The WoodRoom engine wraps it as a
shared, fully wet reverb with smoothed controls, output filtering and guards.
Source propagation and moving early reflections are implemented separately.

The current default build uses Tide Room's independent ClearRoom engine.
The Airwindows-based WoodRoom engine remains available in the source with
TIDE_NATIVE_WOOD=ON and TIDE_CLEAR_ROOM=OFF.
